About Mark'd

A private library for what you actually like.

Our Mission

Mark'd keeps a private record of what you like. A score and a note stay with the moment, so you can look back at your own taste.

The library is for you. It is not there for an audience.

What We Stand For

Privacy First

Logs, photos, ratings, and notes stay on your device. If you turn on iCloud, they sync to your personal iCloud account. We do not sell that data, and the app does not show ads.

Your library

Records stay in your library. There is no feed, and there is no public average.

Keep what matters

You do not have to log everything. Keep the meal, the bottle, or the place you want to find again.

How It Works

A record takes one moment.

  • Point the camera at a barcode, or take a photo.
  • Add your score and a short note.
  • The record stays on your device, and in iCloud if you turn sync on.
  • Make a share card only when you want to send one.

Behind Mark'd

Mark'd is made by a small team. It is a private library, not a social network.
